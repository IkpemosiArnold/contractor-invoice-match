Checking

Ready means the invoice matches one job in the log, nothing has been invoiced against that job yet, the job is marked Completed, the flat is the same, the net and the hours are inside the quote, the lines add up, and VAT is only charged with a VAT number on the invoice.

Anything else is held back with the reason, the invoice words and the log cells. Nothing posts until a person approves it.
